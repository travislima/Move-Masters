Frequently Asked Questions

How much does furniture removal cost in Port Elizabeth?
Every move is priced on the volume of furniture, the distance between the two addresses, and access such as stairs or narrow driveways. Send us a WhatsApp with a few photos of what needs to move and we'll send you a free, no-obligation quote — usually the same day.
Do you transport a single item, like one couch or a fridge?
Yes — single item transport is one of our most popular services in Gqeberha. Whether it's a bed, fridge, washing machine or something you bought on Facebook Marketplace, we collect and deliver it safely for a flat rate rather than a full removal fee.
Which areas of Gqeberha do you cover?
All of Port Elizabeth and Gqeberha, including Summerstrand, Humewood, Walmer, Newton Park, Lorraine, Mill Park, Kabega Park, Sherwood, Framesby, Charlo, Bluewater Bay, Motherwell, Korsten and Bethelsdorp — plus Despatch and Kariega (Uitenhage). Longer Eastern Cape trips are quoted on request.
Will you really buy or sell my unwanted furniture?
We sell your unwanted items on your behalf, or make you an offer on the spot for pieces we can move ourselves. It's an easy way to clear a garage, a flat or an estate and walk away with cash instead of a dumping fee.
Do you remove garden refuse and black bags?
Yes. We load and legally dispose of garden refuse, tree cuttings, grass, black bags and general household junk anywhere in Nelson Mandela Bay. You don't need to lift anything — our team does the loading.
